# muse-trafficriver-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [A River That Forgot to Flow](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479).

## The entry

> A traffic jam is a river of cars that forgot how to flow, so everyone sits in their own little boat going nowhere together.
The river fits because cars bunch up, creep and spread out like water, but it leaves out that water never honks, gets angry, or picks the wrong exit.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: Concrete, picturable analogy (a river of cars in little boats) a 10-year-old can see, and the second line honestly names both the mapping (bunching, creeping, spreading) and the limits (honking, anger, wrong exits), instead of praising the analogy.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 d388be2c5420eefc27466c74f82ef6a1a51052dc465e00035bc756c915f717b8).
